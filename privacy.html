Privacy Policy

Trust No Floor · last updated 7 November 2026

Trust No Floor doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, achievements and death counts are saved only on your device (in the app, or in your browser's local storage on the web). Deleting the app or clearing the browser's site data removes them. "Reset progress" in Settings erases them too.

Game Center

In the iOS app, if you're signed in to Game Center, the game sends your achievements and leaderboard scores (furthest level, total deaths, and fewest deaths for finishing all levels) to Apple's Game Center so they appear there and on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we don't receive it. You can turn Game Center off in the Settings app.

Purchases

The optional full-game unlock is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple ID.

Children

Because the game collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
